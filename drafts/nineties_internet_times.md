# Nineties Internet Times

So I was a [1980s teenage programmer]. In 1991 I went to university,
in Utrecht, the Netherlands. I was barely 18. It was the first time
I lived away from my parents. I didn't know anything. It was a
transformative period in my life.

Let's talk about the Internet as I first got to know it, in the
1990s. This is not a technical story but an intensively personal one.
