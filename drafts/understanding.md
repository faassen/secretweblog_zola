## Running Ahead of our Understanding

Like other forms of reuse, AI lets us run ahead of our human understanding. With
good abstractions this isn't so bad; as long as I understand the outside rules
established by the abstractions, I can use them, no matter how they're
implemented. With copy & paste, it's worse - if I don't take the time to
understand what I'm copying and pasting, I'm gotting to hit a wall sooner rather
than later.

With AI, you hit the wall later. You can run quite far ahead of your human
understanding. The AI confidently proposes next steps, and seems to know what
it's doing, but it tends to be like this no matter whether the next step is
actually possible or not.
